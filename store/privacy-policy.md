# Privacy Policy — Universal Remote (WianTribe)

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Universal Remote ("the app") is published by WianTribe. This policy explains
what the app does and does not do with your information.

## The short version
The app has no account and no cloud service. It controls TVs directly on your
own Wi-Fi network. It does not collect, store, or transmit your personal
information to WianTribe or to anyone else.

## What the app accesses, and why
• Local network. The app finds and controls TVs on your Wi-Fi. This traffic
  stays on your network and goes only to the TVs you choose.
• Microphone (voice search). When you hold the microphone button, your speech
  is turned into text by your device's own speech-recognition service and used
  to search on the TV. The app does not record or keep this audio, and does not
  send it to WianTribe. How the recognition itself is handled is governed by your
  device operating system's privacy terms.
• Microphone (listening features). Audio-streaming features move sound between
  your own devices on your own network or private connection. This audio is not
  sent to WianTribe and is not stored by the app.

## What the app does NOT do
• No account, no login, no profile.
• No advertising and no third-party analytics.
• No selling or sharing of personal data.
• No collection of contacts, location, photos, or browsing history.

## Data retention
The only thing the app saves is the list of TVs you have paired, and the name
you give each one. This is stored on your device and is removed when you remove
a TV or uninstall the app.

## Children
The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children.

## Additional TV platform pairing

Google TV/Android TV pairing generates a client certificate and private key on
your phone. VIDAA pairing stores the TV-issued access/refresh tokens. These are
kept in Android encrypted storage or the iOS Keychain and are used only to
authenticate to your selected TV. TV model and reported firmware versions may be
saved locally for compatibility information. Forgetting a TV removes its saved
platform authorization. No TV pairing credentials are sent to WianTribe servers.
