## Full description (max 4000)
Universal Remote turns your phone into a fast, private remote control for the
TVs on your home Wi-Fi — no account, no sign-up, no ads.

WHAT IT DOES
• Control Roku and Roku TVs over Wi-Fi: D-pad, OK, back, home, volume, power.
• Launch apps and switch HDMI inputs straight from your phone.
• Type with your phone's keyboard instead of pecking letters on screen.
• Voice search — hold the mic button and say what you're looking for.
• Private listening — send the TV's audio to your headphones through the phone,
  so you can watch without disturbing anyone.
• Wake a TV that's turned off, by Wi-Fi, without getting up.
• Add a TV by typing its IP address when automatic discovery can't see it.
• Rename a TV to something you'll actually say — "living room TV", "bedroom TV".

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
There is no account and no cloud. The app talks directly to your TVs on your
own network. It collects no personal information and shows no ads.

Roku is supported today. Samsung and LG are in progress.
